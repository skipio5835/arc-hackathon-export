Operations

Payment LinksCreate a reusable wallet-signed payment request. InvoicesCreate and manage invoice payment flows. SettlementSettle, refund, or record a merchant payout. Revenue SplitExecute configured multi-party allocation. TreasuryReview balances and transfer supported stablecoins. Cross-chain RouterUse Circle App Kit Bridge and CCTP for USDC routing. Stablecoin FXRequest a testnet USDC and EURC App Kit Swap quote.

Wallet safety

Every value-moving action requires a MetaMask confirmation. This application never requests private keys or entity secrets. App Kit Swap requires a user-provided test Kit Key and does not save it.
